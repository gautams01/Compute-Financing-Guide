# Compute Financing Guide

### [▶ Open the interactive dashboard](https://gautams01.github.io/Compute-Financing-Guide/compute-financing-guide.html)

How to pay for AI compute: where the capital comes from, how to make a GPU or data center deal bankable, and what each way of getting compute actually returns.

One self-contained HTML file. No install. Open it in a browser and change any input.

## What's inside

**Financing tree.** Every source of capital for GPUs and data centers, from VC and vendor financing to GPU-backed loans, SPV project bonds and ABS, plus the credit enhancements that make them work: backstops, prepayments, take-or-pay contracts, reserves, insurance and PPAs. Also covers federal, state and public incentives (bonus depreciation, R&D credit, sales tax exemptions, opportunity zones).

**Procurement comparison.** Four ways to get compute, side by side on capital, speed, control, cost, risk, financing route and who to call:

| Rent from cloud | Colo + own GPUs | Modular data center | Own powered shell |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days to start, highest per-hour cost | Months, lower cost, you carry GPU residual risk | Months, cheap or stranded power | 1–3 years, lowest cost at scale |

**Interactive models.** A live per-GPU model for each structure, plus a tab comparing all four. Each one shows returns, breakeven, the cash flow schedule and one-way and two-way sensitivity grids.

| Model | Base case |
|---|---|
| Rent from cloud | 59% IRR on upfront cash; breaks even at 49% utilization |
| Colo + own GPUs | 29.2% project IRR, 110% equity IRR |
| Modular data center | 23.1% project IRR, 82.7% equity IRR |
| Turnkey data center | 23.6% project IRR; 110% equity IRR with an IG tenant, 46.5% with HY |

---

*Defaults are illustrative. Market data, programs and company lists are as of October 2026.*
